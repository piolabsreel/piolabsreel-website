CASE STUDIES

Bonitas Treats - Product Advertising

Service: Video Production | Platform: Instagram

Bonitas Treats needed a short and elegant product video to showcase their tasty Banana, Coconut Crunch Snack. They wanted something Instagram-ready, visually rich, and emotionally warm — something that felt like a hug in video form.

We shot a cozy 45-second promo using soft daylight lighting, a clean table setup, and quick-motion cut-ins of ingredients, all paired with a gentle music bed.

Result: The video helped introduce people to the product, increased engagement significantly, and was reposted by local food bloggers and brand fans.
