CASE STUDIES

King Tutt – “Excessive” Music Video

*Organic Results, no paid ads on social media*

Service: Music Video Production | Platform: YouTube

King Tutt needed a bold, urban performance visual that matched the energy and extravagance of his song “Excessive.” We shot across gritty locations with lighting setups to create a “larger-than-life” feel.

With a Miami-inspired color grade, fast pacing, and cinematic camera motion, the final video captured the full atmosphere of the track and artist’s persona.

❤️ 364+ Likes
💬 40+ Comments
